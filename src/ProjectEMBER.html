About Project Ember

Advancing the future of solar thermal energy

Our Mission

Project Ember is dedicated to developing cutting-edge Organic Rankine Cycle (ORC) technology for solar thermal applications. Our research focuses on creating more efficient, reliable, and cost-effective solar thermal panels that can generate electricity from solar heat with unprecedented efficiency.

Why ORC Technology?

Organic Rankine Cycle technology offers significant advantages over traditional solar thermal systems. By using organic fluids with lower boiling points, our panels can operate efficiently at lower temperatures, making them more practical for residential and commercial applications.

Our Technology

Innovative ORC-based solar thermal solutions

Low-Temperature Operation

Our ORC system operates efficiently at temperatures as low as 80°C, making it suitable for various applications and climates.

Eco-Friendly Fluids

Using environmentally safe organic working fluids that are non-toxic and have minimal environmental impact.

Thermal Storage

Integrated thermal storage capabilities allow for continuous power generation even when the sun isn't shining.

Smart Monitoring

Advanced sensors and control systems optimize performance and provide real-time monitoring and diagnostics.

Modular Design

Scalable modular architecture allows for easy installation and maintenance across different system sizes.

Reliability

Proven ORC technology with enhanced durability and long-term performance guarantees.

Research & Development

Pushing the boundaries of solar thermal technology

Phase 1: Foundation

Establishing the theoretical framework and conducting initial feasibility studies for ORC integration in solar thermal systems.

Phase 2: Prototyping

Developing and testing prototype systems to validate performance metrics and identify optimization opportunities.

Phase 3: Optimization

Refining system components and improving efficiency through advanced materials and control algorithms.

Phase 4: Commercialization

Scaling up production and preparing for market deployment with industry partners.
